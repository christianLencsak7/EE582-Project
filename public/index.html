UNDER THE HOOD

Simple rules.
Visible reasoning.

A transparent heuristic for learning channel selection. No AI model runs inside this tool.

01

Weight the received power

Convert dBm to linear relative power: 10^((RSSI + 50) / 10). A −50 dBm signal has weight 1. A signal 10 dB stronger has ten times the weight.

02

Account for channel overlap

max(0, 1 − |candidate − neighbor| / 5). Nearby channels contribute more. This triangular function is a project assumption, not an IEEE spectral mask.

03

Compare channels 1, 6 and 11

Sum power × overlap for each candidate. Choose a minimum; retain your current channel if it ties. Otherwise, the lowest-numbered minimum breaks the tie.

A model for learning, not a site survey

Scores use arbitrary relative units. They do not predict speed, latency, utilization, or collision probability. Real Wi-Fi also depends on traffic, contention, hidden nodes, non-Wi-Fi interference and propagation. This demo uses channels 1–11, fixed 20 MHz operation, and synthetic neighbors; it does not scan or control your network.
